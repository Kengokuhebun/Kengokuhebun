SECTION 4: STEPS

STEP A — AVATAR CARD (for each of A–D)
Who they are; the exact daily moment; what they've tried and why it failed (ladder, with quotes); what they're afraid of; what they secretly want; their words for the problem (5–10 phrases); where they scroll.

STEP B — BURNING DESIRE TEST (PASS / FAIL)
Pass only with: present-tense detail, a list of things already tried, "has anyone found anything that works?", "same" replies, and the problem tied to something bigger (work, relationship, sleep, the child's development). Give 3 linked threads as proof.

STEP C — TAM, per country (AU, US, UK, CA) and combined
Group size (cite official source + year) × share with the problem (cite) × share for whom it's burning (conservative, [ESTIMATE]) = addressable households per year. Count households, not children. Bar: 500,000/year. Report AU alone and all four combined.

STEP D — META SATURATION
Search the Meta Ad Library (AU first, then US/UK) for "screen free", "instead of screens", "iPad", "screen time" + toys/kids. Who targets each avatar? How many active ads? Longest runtime (30+ days = proven)? Quote the top hooks verbatim with the advertiser name. Is anyone selling an LED/light-up drawing board on a screen-time angle? Rating 1 (everyone targets them) to 5 (nobody does).

STEP E — PRODUCT FIT FROM REVIEWS (the most important new step)
Read at least 150 reviews of LED/light-up drawing boards (Amazon AU/US/UK, Kmart AU, Big W, Walmart, Temu/AliExpress listings with reviews). Count and quote:
- How long kids stay engaged (minutes/days/weeks); how many mention novelty wearing off.
- How many mention it replacing a screen, iPad or TV.
- Child ages mentioned; any "too young" or "too old".
- Mess, marker drying, marker staining, durability, light failure, charging.
- Bedtime or night-light use, and any sleep concerns about the light.
- Who bought it (parent, grandparent, gift) and for what occasion.
Give counts out of the total read. Then rate fit per avatar: STRONG / MODERATE / WEAK.

STEP F — SCORE (/25), AU-only and four-country

Hard override: below 500k or a burning-desire FAIL cannot be rated above TEST. Also flag WEAK product fit.

STEP G — ANGLE FOR THE TOP 2
