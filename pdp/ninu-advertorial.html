I actually do my scalp oil every night now. That wasn't true six months ago.

I had the castor oil. I had the rosemary. I believed in the routine. I just couldn't make myself do it consistently.

The routine I could never keep up with

I'd do it four nights in a row and then the baby would be up three times and I'd get into bed with wet hands and just not do it. Then I'd skip a second night. The bottle would migrate to the back of the shelf and I'd tell myself I'd start over Sunday. I never started over Sunday.

The thing I kept reading about, the consistent nightly scalp massage, the women who said they did it every single night for six months, I couldn't figure out how they were doing it. My routine was annoying. Oil on my fingers before I touched anything else. Running down my neck. Soaking into the towel I'd wrapped around my shoulders to protect my shirt. By the time I finished one section I was already thinking about the cleanup. It stopped feeling like self-care and started feeling like a chore.

A chore at 10pm after a full day with a baby is a chore that gets skipped.
