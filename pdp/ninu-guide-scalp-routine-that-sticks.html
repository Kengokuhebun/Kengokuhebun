NINUWhy routines fail
The pattern

Routines often wobble around week two.

You do it four nights in a row. Then a rough night comes. You get into bed with wet hands and skip it. You skip a second night. The bottle drifts to the back of the shelf, and you tell yourself you’ll start over on Sunday.

Nothing in that story is a character flaw. It’s a routine with too many steps, in the wrong place, at the wrong time.

The habit was always there. The routine just needed to be easier than the reason to quit.

Three things help a routine survive a hard week:

Easy

Fewer steps, set up in advance, one hand.

Cued

Tied to something you already do every night.

Forgiving

A missed night doesn’t reset anything.

The rest of this guide builds those three into your evening.

NINUThe routine
Two minutes

Your nightly routine.

1

Keep it where your hand goes

Leave your dispenser and oil on the nightstand, in your bedside pouch. If you have to go and find it, you won’t.

2

Fill

Fill the clear chamber with your oil. The 1 to 4ml marks show you how much. About 2ml a night means one 60ml bottle lasts around a month.

3

Part

Part your hair so the tips can reach your scalp.

4

Press

Press the hollow silicone tips against your scalp along the part. The oil is released right there, on the skin. One hand.

5

Move across

Make a new part and repeat. Work across the top of your head, a part at a time.

6

Bonnet, then bed

Satin bonnet on, light off. Tick your night in the tracker.

The tired-night version

Fill. One part. Bonnet. Tick the night. A smaller routine you do beats a bigger one you skip.

NINUMake it stick
Easy. Cued. Forgiving.

Build it into your evening.

Pick your cue

Attach the routine to something you already do every night. Finish the sentence: “After I…”

  • …brush my teeth
  • …do the last feed or the last tidy
  • …plug in my phone
  • …put my pyjamas on

Make it easy

Set up once, then leave it. Dispenser filled, pouch on the nightstand, bonnet next to it. The fewer decisions at 10pm, the better.

Plan the hard night

If the baby’s been up and I’m wrecked, then I do the tired-night version. One part counts. Tick the night.

Never miss twice

Missing a night happens to everyone. What matters is tonight. Your tracker doesn’t reset when you miss, and neither should you.

NINUWash day and oil basics
The extras

Wash day, and a few basics.

On wash day

Use your silicone scalp massager while you shampoo. Gentle circles, light pressure, then rinse as normal. It’s a quiet minute that’s just for you. Don’t scrub hard.

Your oil

Use an oil you like. Most thin scalp oils work in the dispenser. The dispenser is the tool; the oil is your choice.

Safety, briefly

  • Patch test first. Dab a little on your inner arm and wait 24 hours before your first use.
  • Stop if it irritates. Rinse off and don’t use it again.
  • Pregnant or nursing? Strong oils, rosemary included, are worth checking with your doctor first.
  • Keep oil away from your eyes.

Your routine doesn’t change your wash schedule. Wash your hair as you normally would.

NINUYour 30 nights
The plan

Four weeks, one focus each.

Week 1Nights 1 to 7

Make it effortless

Set up the kit where your hand goes. Do the routine at roughly the same time each night. Don’t aim for perfect. Aim for done.

Week 2Nights 8 to 14

Anchor it

Pick your cue and stick to it. “After I brush my teeth” works because the teeth are already on the list.

Week 3Nights 15 to 21

Plan the hard nights

Write your “if, then” down. Use the tired-night version without guilt. Notice how many nights you’ve ticked.

Week 4Nights 22 to 30

Make it yours

Around night 25, check how much oil is left in the bottle. Keep what’s working, change what isn’t, and decide how you want your routine to look from here.

Your Night Tracker shows all 30 nights at a glance. Tap a night to tick it. Missed one? Nothing resets.

NINUQuestions
Quick answers

Things people ask.

How much oil do I use each night?
About 2ml. A 60ml bottle lasts around 30 nights at that rate. If you’re using more, pull back.
Can I use my own oil?
Most thin scalp oils work. Try one you already like.
Will my hair be greasy?
The oil goes along your part rather than all over your strands, so you use a small amount. If your hair looks oily, use less.
Do I wash it out in the morning?
Wash your hair as you normally would. Nothing about the routine changes your wash days.
What if I miss a night?
Then you miss a night. Nothing resets. Do tonight’s routine and tick it.
It’s taking longer than two minutes.
You’re probably doing more than you need to. Try the tired-night version for a few nights and build back up.
