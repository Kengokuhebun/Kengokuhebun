# Ninu — PDP Gallery Image Prompts (v2, matched to actual product)

## Product identity (bake this into every prompt exactly)

Ninu is a small, upright cylindrical scalp oil dispenser, roughly the size of a shot glass. It has three distinct parts:

1. **Top:** a smooth, matte white plastic dome cap (like an inverted rounded lid).
2. **Middle:** a fully transparent cylindrical chamber that holds the oil, with fine engraved measurement markings on the side (1ml, 2ml, 3ml, 4ml). You can see the oil level through it.
3. **Bottom applicator:** a cluster of 6–8 short, translucent, hollow silicone prongs pointing downward. These are **hollow applicator tubes**, not bristles — each one dispenses a thin stream of oil directly onto the scalp when pressed against skin. They look like tiny clear droppers arranged in a rosette.
4. **Base cap:** a matching matte white plastic cap that snaps onto the bottom to cover the applicator prongs when stored.

**Overall aesthetic:** clean, clinical, modern, minimal — closer to Apple accessory or Muji than crunchy-natural. White + clear + subtle warm shadows. No logo shown in most shots (add a tiny wordmark in the hero only if requested).

**Model target:** Nano Banana Pro. Same prompts work for Midjourney v6/v7 with light phrasing tweaks.

**Reference image needed:** ✅ = MUST run as image-to-image with your master hero as the visual reference. ❌ = can be generated straight from text.
