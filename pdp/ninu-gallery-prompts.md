# Ninu — PDP Gallery Image Prompts

**Product identity to bake into every prompt:** the Ninu dispenser is a slim, ergonomic, palm-sized bottle with a soft dome of stainless-steel bristles at the tip (like a scalp massager brush, but sealed to a chamber holding scalp oil). Body is matte cream ceramic-look plastic with a thin brushed-copper collar where the bristle dome meets the body. Label: minimal wordmark "N I N U" in serif, tracked wide, in dark espresso ink. Warm cream + terracotta + soft copper palette throughout the campaign.

**Model:** written for Nano Banana Pro. Same prompts work for Midjourney with minor tweaks.

**Reference image needed:** ✅ = must be run as image-to-image (or with a product reference upload) once you have your first hero product photo. ❌ = can be generated straight from text.

## Suggested gallery order (5-image PDP set)

Elixire-inspired PDPs run best with 5 slots. Recommended:

1. **Image 1** (Hero product) — first thumbnail, sets the brand
2. **Image 3** (Bristle macro with oil bead) — sells the mechanism
3. **Image 4** or **Image 2** (Ritual/in-hand) — humanizes it
4. **Image 5** (Kit flat-lay) — shows the whole offer including the bonnet bonus
5. **Image 6** (Before/after) — the conversion asset; keep as the last thumbnail so it's the payoff

Images 7 and 8 are extras for landing pages, email, and Meta ads.

## Workflow

1. Generate Image 1 first (no reference needed). Iterate until the dispenser silhouette + bristle dome + label look correct.
2. Save that as your master reference.
3. Feed it into Higgsfield / Nano Banana Pro as image reference for Images 2, 3, 5, 7.
4. Images 4, 6, 8 can be generated directly from text — either the product isn't the focal point, or there's no product.
