Questions parents ask

What age is it for?

Recommended for ages 3+. Younger children should be supervised with the markers.

Isn’t a glowing board just another screen?

No. There’s no app, no internet and nothing to watch. The light only makes your child’s own drawing glow, and it does nothing until they pick up a marker.

Will the board scratch or smear?

Use only the included markers and wipe with a soft damp cloth, not paper towel or anything abrasive. Let it dry before the next drawing and it stays clear.

Do the markers wipe off easily?

Yes. A soft damp cloth clears the board. Keep the caps on between drawings so the markers don’t dry out.

How is it powered?

By the USB cable, from a phone charger, laptop or power bank. No batteries needed.

Can it be used as a night light?

Yes. Many families leave a drawing up and use the glow for the bedtime wind-down.
