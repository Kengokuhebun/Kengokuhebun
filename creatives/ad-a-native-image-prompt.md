# Ad A — Native image prompt (temple check)

Built against the Phase 5 Native Image Psychology protocol.

## Protocol check

**Core question:** If this mom grabbed her phone right now to document her reality, what would she photograph?

**The sentence from the copy that stops the scroll:**
> "Before I check my phone, before I check the baby monitor, I'm at the bathroom mirror pressing my hair back to check my temples."

That sentence is about *the moment she's alone with it* **plus** *the evidence no one else sees*. So the image is: her own right temple, in the bathroom mirror, taken from first-person POV with her left hand pressing her hair back out of the way. Harsh overhead bathroom light. Phone-camera quality. No performed emotion. Just the quiet documentation of a thinning hairline at 6am.

**Would this image make sense posted to Reddit with "this is what month 5 postpartum looks like" as the caption?** Yes.

---

## The prompt (for Nano Banana Pro / GPT Image / Midjourney)

```
A raw, unposed amateur smartphone selfie taken in a bathroom mirror from first-person POV, early morning, overhead vanity lighting (slightly harsh and yellow-tinged). A woman in her early 30s, mid-length dark brown hair pulled back loosely at the nape, is pressing her left hand against the side of her head to flatten her hair out of the way and expose her right temple hairline. The camera is her phone held in her right hand at chest level, framing tight on her face from the eyebrow line up to the top of her ear so her temple fills about 40 percent of the frame.

Her right temple shows visible postpartum thinning: the hairline has receded slightly and there is a sparse half-moon of exposed scalp behind the temple with wispy, see-through baby hairs instead of a solid edge. The skin beneath looks pale and slightly pink from being pressed.

She wears a faded oversized grey sleep tee, one strap of a nursing bra visible under the collar. Her face is clean, no makeup, faint under-eye shadows, lips neutral. No performed expression — she is simply looking at the thing she is documenting, not at the camera.

Background behind her in the mirror: a cream subway-tile wall, the corner of a white medicine cabinet with the door slightly ajar, a muslin burp cloth draped over the shower rod in the far background out of focus, a bottle of baby shampoo on the counter edge barely in frame. Mirror has faint toothpaste flecks near the lower edge.

Shot style: iPhone selfie camera, slightly soft focus, no filter, no beauty retouching, natural grain, slight motion blur on the pressing hand. Lighting is unflattering overhead vanity bulb that casts a hard shadow under her jaw. Composition is slightly off-center, like someone took the photo quickly and didn't recompose. Zero styling, zero glamour, zero stock-photo polish.

The image should look like something a mother at month five postpartum would text to her sister with no caption, as evidence. Not a product photo. Not an ad. A document.

Aspect ratio: 1:1 square, 1080x1080. (Also generate 9:16 vertical at 1080x1920 and 1.91:1 landscape at 1200x628 by recomposing with the temple as the dominant element in each frame.)
```

---

## What NOT to let the model do

- No smiling, no sad face, no performed expression. Phase 5: "Faces performing sadness looks like acting."
- No visible product. Phase 5: "Show the problem moment, not the solution."
- No overlaid text, labels, arrows, or callouts. The copy explains, the image shows.
- No studio or ring light. Overhead bathroom vanity only.
- No clean, styled bathroom. Signs of real postpartum life only (burp cloth, baby shampoo, nursing bra strap).

## If the model keeps making it look like a stock photo

Add to the prompt: *"Flaw-first photography. Think the kind of photo someone takes to text their doctor, not the one they'd post on Instagram. Visible pores, uneven skin, hair out of place, imperfect framing. The point of the photo is the thinning temple, not the person."*
