# R27: Native image set for the adapted swipe copy (master doc native protocol)

Written 2026-11-06. Follows the master document's "Native Image Ad: Master Prompt": dissect the copy, map its arc, 20 ideas across the 5 native styles (plus hybrids), tagged to a moment and a line of copy, then the 5 most likely to perform ("pain exaggerated, worst state"). The protocol's rules are applied as written.

**Rules from the protocol (non-negotiable):**
1. **No product in the image.** No Ninu, no packaging, no logo, no brand colours. The product lives in the copy. (A plain, unlabeled amber bottle is a prop, not the product.)
2. **Phone-camera quality.** No studio light or polish.
3. **Real people, real settings.** I keep people out of frame (hands, backs, rooms), so nothing looks like a model.
4. **Looks native.** Nothing signals "advertisement".
5. **Each image earns its place.** Tied to a moment and a line of copy.
6. **Accurate, not fabricated.** No before and after of results, no fake proof. Diagrams are illustrative.
7. **The image is the hook; the copy is the sell.**
8. **Variety** across the 20.

Ninu guardrails on top: no hair thinning, hair loss, scalp close-ups or regrowth imagery. Nothing is a customer photo and nothing is labelled as one.

**Deviation from the protocol, on purpose.** The protocol says to run it once per longform copy. Your 10 copies share one avatar, one arc and the same beats, so I ran it once across the whole set and pair images to copies at the end (section 5). If you want the full 20 for any single copy, say which.

## 1. Dissect (shared across all the copies)

- **Core problem:** a scalp oil routine that dies within days. Physically, it's mess (oil on fingers, neck, towel, pillowcase). Emotionally, it's skipping and guilt.
- **Emotional tension:** guilt ("I can't even do this one thing for myself"), exhaustion, self-blame. Secondary: embarrassment about the bottle at the back of the shelf, and the feeling of starting over every Sunday.
- **Turning point:** the towel test, or the question "where is the oil actually going?"
- **Mechanism (problem):** a dropper puts oil on hair, fingers put it on hands, neck and towel. Little reaches the scalp.
- **Mechanism (solution):** hollow silicone tips pressed along the part release oil on the skin. One hand, about two minutes. (Not shown in images, per rule 1.)
- **Dream state:** a routine that takes two minutes, no towel, no cleanup, no starting over on Sunday.
- **Avatar:** a tired postpartum mom, about 34, a baby around five months old, a husband, a bathroom she uses at 10pm.
- **Setting details:** edge of the tub, towel on the shoulders, wet hands in bed, a baby monitor, a bathroom shelf, a nightstand, a drawer of oils, a shower cap, phone reminders.
- **Proof points:** none to show as results. The only proof is a test she can run herself, the towel test.

## 2. The arc (7 beats)

1. Hook: "Baby up 3 times. Wet hands, in bed. Skipped my scalp oil again."
2. Problem: four nights, then a skip, the bottle drifts to the back
3. Escalation: the towel test (part barely damp, fingers slick, towel soaked)
4. Discovery: "where is the oil actually going?"
5. Mechanism reveal: the route, not the bottle
6. Dream state: the first night with no towel; week five automatic
7. Close: what it won't do, the kit, the test, "make tonight the easy one"

## 3. The 20 ideas

Mix: 4 Visceral, 3 Real-Moment, 3 Scientific, 3 Before/After and POV, 3 Headline, 4 Hybrid. The "Prompt" under each is a paste-ready image prompt (natural language, lighting first). Ideas marked "spec" or "headline" need HTML for the text, because image generators garble small words.

**Tail to append to every generation prompt** (already included in each below):
`Shot on an iPhone, ordinary phone sharpness, slight noise, imperfect framing. No text, no logos, no brand names, no packaging, no product, no faces.`

Aspect ratio 4:5 for the feed and 9:16 for Stories, set in the generator, not in the prompt. Resolution 2k. Generate 4 variations and choose the least glossy.

## 6. How to test them

- One variable per round: same copy, headline, description, CTA and audience; only the image changes.
- Start with **image 2 on the R16 body** (it matches the winning Hook3 words). Then image 1.
- At your budget that's one image test at a time. Read link CPC first, then add-to-cart per click, then purchases. Treat anything under about 1,000 impressions as noise.
- Don't change the page during an image test.

## 7. What this does not cover (next)

- **The free-gifts offer images** (what's in the box, the gift tiles) show the real products and gifts. They belong on the page, not in natives (rule 1), and need real photos once the massager and pouch are sourced.
- **The oil PDP images.** They have to match the bottle that actually ships. I need the supplier's photo or a sample of the bottle and its ingredient list before I can write those prompts.
- **The bundle block images** follow the same rule: only real, photographed items.
