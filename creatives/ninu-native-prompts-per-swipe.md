# Native image prompts: one per long-form swipe

Paste each prompt into your image generator. Each image goes with the matching copy in `ninu-swipe-copy-paste-ready.md`.

**Note:** the two prompts with a body in frame (5 and 8) say it is a woman, with feminine hands and shoulders. If one still comes out male, add "woman, female, feminine" at the start.

**What changed:** every prompt now says the oil is a pale golden-yellow, clear rosemary-style oil (like olive oil), never dark or motor oil, and every scene is greasy, sticky and uncomfortable on purpose, to get an instant "ew" reaction.

**Settings for every one:** 4:5 for the feed, 2k, generate 4 versions and pick the most disgusting yet believable one. If the oil still comes out dark, add "honey-coloured, light and transparent" after the oil sentence. No product, logo or text is in any of them. The image stops the scroll, and the copy does the selling.

*The oil patch on the pillowcase.*

```

A tight crop on the corner of a white cotton pillowcase with a huge greasy patch of pale golden-yellow oil, the cotton gone see-through and clingy, a shiny pooled sheen in the middle and a yellow tinted ring creeping outward at the edges, a second smaller slick near the seam. The corner of a rumpled grey duvet beside it, with a shiny smear on it. It looks soaked, sticky and gross. Slightly grainy and warm, shallow depth of field like phone portrait mode. The oil is a pale golden-yellow, clear and translucent like olive oil or a light rosemary-infused oil, with a greasy wet shine. It is NOT dark, brown or black and not motor oil. Where it soaks fabric the fabric turns darker, clingy and see-through with a yellow tint. Shot on an iPhone, ordinary phone sharpness, slight noise, imperfect framing. No text, no logos, no brand names, no packaging, no product, no faces.
```
