# Native image prompts: one per long-form swipe

Paste each prompt into your image generator. Each image goes with the matching copy in `ninu-swipe-copy-paste-ready.md`.

**Note:** the two prompts with a body in frame (5 and 8) say it is a woman, with feminine hands and shoulders. If one still comes out male, add "woman, female, feminine" at the start.

**What changed:** every prompt now says the oil is a pale golden-yellow, clear rosemary-style oil (like olive oil), never dark or motor oil, and every scene is greasy, sticky and uncomfortable on purpose, to get an instant "ew" reaction.

**Settings for every one:** 4:5 for the feed, 2k, generate 4 versions and pick the most disgusting yet believable one. If the oil still comes out dark, add "honey-coloured, light and transparent" after the oil sentence. No product, logo or text is in any of them. The image stops the scroll, and the copy does the selling.
