# Ninu green-screen UGC: 3 hooks, one body

Format: a woman talks to camera, cut out and placed in the bottom corner (green-screen style). Behind her, full-screen stills and clips show exactly what she is saying. 9:16, captions on, about 60 seconds.

**Only the hook changes.** The body is locked and identical in all three, so the test reads the hook and nothing else (master doc: one variable per cell). Each hook opens a different door: pain, mechanism, identity.

**Rules applied:** behaviour and delivery claims only. No hair results, no shedding, no "hair doesn't absorb oil". No em dashes, no exclamation marks, no hedges. Price lines are true at any date (no Nov 10 deadline). No free gifts until they are in the box. The presenter is AI, so the video carries a small on-screen label: **Dramatisation**. She tells the brand story; she is not presented as a customer review.

| 4. Talking video | **Kling AI Avatar 2.0 Standard**, 720P | Image + voice in, lip-synced video out, up to 5 minutes per take, cheapest per second that runs the full minute in one take | $0.0505 a second |
| 5. Motion on stills | CapCut slow zoom (free); Veo 3.1 Lite 720P only if a shot needs real movement | Stills with a slow push read as native and cost nothing | $0 to $0.15 per clip |

**Save by making the body once.** The body is the same in all three videos, so lip-sync it once (about 57 seconds, $2.90) and make the three hooks as separate 3-second takes ($0.15 each). The cut from hook to body hides under the first full-screen image. Three finished talking heads cost about $3.35 instead of about $9.

**Test first:** a 10-second test line on Kling AI Avatar Standard ($0.51) and on OmniHuman 2.0 ($0.70, turn its watermark off). Keep whichever looks more natural, then run the full body.

**Estimated total for all three videos:** about $6 to $7, including tests and a few re-rolls of images.

Alternatives considered: InfiniteTalk is cheap at 480P but limited to 15 seconds per take, so the body would need 4 stitched takes. OmniHuman 1.5 is the most expensive ($0.135 a second) and recommends under 15 seconds. Veo 3.1 and Kling 3.0 make speech themselves, but in 8 to 15 second clips the voice changes from clip to clip.
