# R8: Hook test (one color, three hooks) + improved mechanism

One variable: **the hook**. Body from the lead onward ("I had the castor oil."), headline,
description, CTA, Orange gradient and hook-only layout are identical on all three ads. Body
includes the rewritten mechanism paragraph (hair doesn't absorb oil, skin does).

Rule from Aarreign's Master Document (Longform Copy section 6 and Avatar Deepening): three hooks
must each use a different pattern and open a different door (pain, identity, curiosity/mechanism).
"Three variations of the same hook is not three hooks." Hook and lead are one unit, so the lead
does not move and each new hook hands off cleanly into it.

## The three hooks

| Ad | Door | Master-doc pattern | Source (from the winning copy) | On-image line | Why it hands off to the lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1_C_Orange_HookOnly (control) | Identity / relief | Transformation story | Control, converted in R1, Orange, Purple | ok i actually do my scalp oil EVERY night now | "That wasn't true six months ago" leads into "I had the castor oil..." |
| A1_C_Orange_HookPain | Pain | Drop them into the scene (sensory loop moment) | P3: "the baby would be up three times and I'd get into bed with wet hands and just not do it" | baby up 3 times. wet hands, in bed. skipped my scalp oil again | The skip is the problem; the lead explains she had everything and still couldn't stay consistent |
| A1_C_Orange_HookMechanism | Curiosity / mechanism | Mistake callout + one-line contradiction | P8: "Not because I'd become more disciplined. Because the tool I'd been using was wrong"; desire = exoneration (R2) | i thought i was bad at scalp oil. i was just doing it wrong | "I wasn't" opens a loop the lead keeps open and P6 ("the wrong place") closes |

No hook adds a claim, detail or situation that isn't already in the converting copy.

## Ad 1: A1_C_Orange_HookOnly (control) · primary text (430 words)

I actually do my scalp oil every night now. That wasn't true six months ago.

I had the castor oil. I had the rosemary. I believed in the routine. I just couldn't make myself do it consistently.

I'd do it four nights in a row and then the baby would be up three times and I'd get into bed with wet hands and just not do it. Then I'd skip a second night. The bottle would migrate to the back of the shelf and I'd tell myself I'd start over Sunday. I never started over Sunday.

The thing I kept reading about, the consistent nightly scalp massage, the women who said they did it every single night for six months, I couldn't figure out how they were doing it. My routine was annoying. Oil on my fingers before I touched anything else. Running down my neck. Soaking into the towel I'd wrapped around my shoulders to protect my shirt. By the time I finished one section I was already thinking about the cleanup. It stopped feeling like self-care and started feeling like a chore.

A chore at 10pm after a full day with a baby is a chore that gets skipped.

Turns out I'd been putting it in the wrong place. Hair doesn't absorb oil. Skin does. Nights of working it down the strands, and almost none of it reached my scalp.

The habit was always there. The routine just needed to be easier than the reason to quit.
