## 5. What's different from your proven copy (and what that means for reproduction)

- **No postpartum-specific framing anywhere, visually or verbally** — this is "any woman with
  hair," not a mother-specific angle. The entire niche specificity in a script like this would
  have to live in the words, not the staging (no baby monitor, no nursery, nothing). That's
  actually convenient: you don't need special set dressing to carry your angle here, just the
  script.
- **Failed Solutions is much shorter proportionally** (≈11% vs your 40%) — spoken + demo format
  compresses this beat naturally, the visual mess does work your paragraph had to do in words.
- **The aphorism/authority line sits mid-video as a bridge, not as the final close** — different
  positioning than your Rule of One line, which sits right before the offer in every proven
  execution.
- **Completely drops price, CTA, and guarantee** — relies entirely on the platform. Your ads have
  never tested that; every proven execution speaks the offer and a two-roads close. Dropping it
  here would be changing two things at once (format + removing a FIXED beat), so the
  reproduction below keeps your offer and CTA spoken, just adapted to natural speech.

## 6. Reproduction script — Ninu's locked content, Kitsch's structure/pacing

Per the Master Doc's Format Translation rule, the content below is not new: Hook (P1),
Lead (P2), the close line (P9), and the offer paragraph (P10) are quoted directly from

**Visual format:** one unbroken handheld selfie take, ordinary bedroom, no cuts, no graphics
