# R9: 3D animation story ad (~1 minute) from the winning Ad C copy

Same proven story, new format: a stylized 3D animated mom tells it in ~64 seconds.
Every VO line is a sentence from the Ad C body that converted (`ninu-r1-long-form.md`, Ad C), lifted
verbatim except where marked. The mechanism paragraph is the ORIGINAL P6, not the R8 rewrite, so
the only things that change are format and length.

Honest note: the long form is 410 words and this is 186. Cutting it to a minute changes length as
well as format, so this tests "does the story work as a 3D animation", not a clean copy test.

An animated character also removes the standing caveat from `ninu-dissect-winner-adC.md` (first-person
copy reading as a real customer testimonial): nobody is presented as a real, verified buyer.

## Style bible (lock this before generating anything)

- **Look:** soft stylized 3D, Pixar-like, tactile materials, shallow depth of field. If the Atria reference
  ad you saved looks different (clay, glossy, flatter), swap this line and keep everything else.
- **Palette:** cream #F5EFE6, espresso #241812, gold #C79A5A for brand moments. Cool night blue for the
  "before" scenes, warm lamp amber for the "after" scenes. The grade shift is the story.
- **Character (Maya):** 32, soft wavy brown hair with a middle part, round friendly features, tired eyes
  in the "before" scenes. Tan robe, same casting as the PDP slide 02 lifestyle image. Make a character sheet
  first (front, 3/4, profile, three expressions) and attach it to every shot.
- **Baby:** never on screen. A crib silhouette and the baby monitor blips carry it.
- **Product fidelity:** the dispenser must match the master product reference: cream dome cap, clear
  reservoir with golden oil and 1-4ml markings, multiple hollow silicone tips at the base. Attach the
  master reference to every shot that shows it (S1, S5, S6, S8, S9). Video models drift small details, so
  keep product close-ups short and check the tip count before accepting a clip.
- **Edit rules (same as your other creatives):** hard cuts only, no crossfades or wipes. Burned-in bold
  white captions with a dark outline. 9:16.
- **Audio:** VO from your separate audio generator with the locked voice (`r7-ugc-avatar-modular-production.md`
  section 6a), not Kling's voice. Kling mispronounced "Ninu" last time. Light warm score under it.

## Shot list (186 words, about 64s at a natural pace)

### S1 · 0:00-0:05 · Hook
**VO (15 words):** I actually do my scalp oil every night now. That wasn't true six months ago.
*Source: P1, verbatim*
**Visual:** Locked medium close-up. A stylized 3D mom (Maya) sits on the edge of a bed at night: satin bonnet on, shoulders relaxed, dispenser resting in one hand, soft smile, warm lamp glow. **Camera:** Slow 3% push-in on both halves. No transition, hard cut only.
**Sound / text:** Soft room tone, one low warm pad. Captions on.

### S2 · 0:05-0:15 · Lead + the skip

**Camera:** Shelf: slow lateral slide. Bed: static medium shot, then slow push to her hands.
**Sound / text:** Three soft monitor blips, a tired exhale.

I actually do my scalp oil every night now. That wasn't true six months ago.

I had the castor oil. I had the rosemary. I just couldn't make myself do it consistently. I'd get into bed with wet hands and just not do it.
