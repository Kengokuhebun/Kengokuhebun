# Ninu Round 1 — Long-Form Primary Text (3 ads)

Built using:
- **Breakthrough Advertising (Schwartz):** Awareness Level 3 + Sophistication Level 3 → lead with MECHANISM not claim; Gradualization (chain of agreements before the reveal); channel mass desire onto the delivery tool.
- **Great Leads (Masterson & Forde):** Ad A = Problem-Solution lead; Ad B = Proclamation lead; Ad C = Story Lead.
- **Kitsch swipe elements** (see `ninu-r1-test-ads.md`): three delivery bullets, mechanism as hero, naming the old mess bluntly, ease as emotional payoff.

These are the full primary text blocks. Each is designed to be copied verbatim into Ads Manager. The first 2-3 lines are the "thumb-stop" — Meta shows that much before "See more."

**BTA annotations:**

**BTA annotations:**

## Pre-launch checks (same as `ninu-r1-test-ads.md`)

- **First-person.** All three read as one mom's story. Run as real UGC or as brand voice. Do not label as a real customer review unless a real customer wrote it.
- **Oil bottle branding.** The MELAO label on the supplier's oil conflicts with the trust frame. Ask the supplier about unbranded or private-label oil before launch.
- **No growth or regrowth claims** anywhere in these three ads. "The habit I couldn't maintain" is a behavior claim, not a hair-outcome claim — keep it that way.
