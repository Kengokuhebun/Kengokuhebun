# Adapted swipe copy: index

Last updated 2026-11-05. This is the one place that lists every swipe that has been rebuilt for Ninu (angle: habit and delivery; desire: "I was never the problem, the method was"; avatar: the postpartum mom). Rebuilds take a reference ad's **structure only**. Claims, invented people and numbers from the references are never carried over.

## How to choose which to run

1. Buyer-language copy first: **R16** against the Ad C control (the current test).
2. Then the rebuilds below, one at a time, as format or structure tests. At your budget, one clean test at a time.
3. Check each against `ninu-core-copy-resource-breakthrough-advertising.md` section 6 before it goes live.

## Rules for every rebuild

- Keep the persuasion mechanics, never the references' claims. No medical or hair-growth claims. No invented authorities, studies, statistics or testimonials.
- Brand-voice first person, unsigned. Not presented as a real customer.
- Price and deadline lines (Nov 10 and $62.49) only if the price really changes.
- Gift lines (massager, pouch, guide, tracker) only when the gifts exist and the guide and tracker emails work.
- No em dashes, exclamation marks or invented scarcity.
- Each ad is one variable against the control: same headline, description, CTA and audience.
