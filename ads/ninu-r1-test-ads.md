## Where each Kitsch element is used

| Working element (source) | How it's used here |

| Fear-of-thinning hook ("Your Bald Spot's Worst Nightmare", Ads 1-2) | Ad A's hook enters through the same fear, in first person ("check my temples"), not "your bald spot" |
| "X's worst nightmare" frame (Ads 1-2) | Ad C's headline and share card: "Your messy oil routine's worst nightmare" (the enemy is the mess, not her body) |
| Three delivery bullets: precise, effortless, scalp not hair (Ads 1-2) | The shared body: Precise / Easy / Less waste, reworded for our hollow tips and ml-marked chamber |
| Short primary text with emoji bullets (Ad 2) | The body is three emoji bullets plus one offer line, not longform |
| "Because of this little gadget, I actually use my ___" (UGC Vid 2) | Ad C's hook |
| Naming the old mess bluntly ("freaking messy," "uneven," "dousing") (Vid 2) | Ad C's lead: "The dropper was messy and my fingers were uneven" |
| "I can do this whenever" ease (Vid 2) | "One hand, a few minutes" in the body; "Make tonight's routine the easy one" in Ad C's CTA |
| Benefit headline under the creative ("Healthy hair starts at the scalp") | Each ad's headline states the delivery benefit |
| Send traffic to the product page, not an advertorial (Ads 1-2) | All three ads go to the same product page |
| Risk reversal (Kitsch: 90-day returns) | 30-day guarantee in the body and in every CTA |

## What I left out on purpose

- "Sold out 5 times," "award-winning," "everyone is raving": no basis for any of them.
- "Improve scalp health," "reduce frizz," "stimulate hair growth": claims we don't make.
- Roller-ball and cooling-massage language: not our mechanism.
- "Your bald spot": implies knowledge of the viewer's body.

## Check before launch

- **First-person leads.** All three read as one mom's own story. They need to be a real person's story, or be run as brand voice. Don't present them as a real customer's testimonial if they aren't.
- **The oil bottle.** The supplier's oil is sold under another brand's label (MELAO). Ask the supplier about unbranded or private-label oil, because a different brand on the bottle works against the trust fix.
- **The URL** in the CSV is the myshopify address. Swap in your custom domain if you connect one.
- **Targeting.** "Broad" in your plan; I set women, 25-44, US, Advantage+ audience on and no interests. Change age and gender if you want it broader.
- **Image slots** are blank, as before. Attach the creative above to each ad after import.
