## Safe-claims notes (supplements)

- The script uses first-person experience only ("I feel steadier"), with no claims to treat, cure or prevent anything. Avoid adding specifics like "fixes adrenal fatigue", "boosts metabolism by X%" or "replaces coffee".
- Meta and TikTok restrict health claims and before/after framing for supplements. Clients will need to supply their own approved claims and disclaimers.
- Label the portfolio piece as an AI-generated concept demo, and follow each platform's AI-content disclosure setting when it's posted or used in ads.
- The generic bottle is a placeholder. When a client brings a real product, swap the Step 1 prompt for their packaging and add letter-by-letter spelling of the brand name.

## QA checklist after generation

1. Bottle stays blank in every shot (no invented label or text).
2. Bottle never covers the mouth during speech.
3. Face matches the portrait panel between beats (hair, freckles, mole).
4. Beat 4 shows exactly three capsules, with no extra spilling.
5. Lip-sync holds on all four spoken lines.
6. If a beat fails, regenerate in two 10s segments (Beats 1–3 and Beats 4–5) using the same two reference images.
