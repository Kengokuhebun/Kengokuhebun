# Competitor LED-drawing-board video ad swipes — cross-ad index

Five winning paid-social video ads, each 50+ days of runtime per the user. All five sell the **same product category** to the **same deep desire** (a gift kids will love and use), with structural variations.

Specs in this folder, each a full Dissect Winner teardown:
- `ad-spec-V1-gomymo-gift-ugc.md` — English, 47.6s, UGC parent voice, scarcity close
- `ad-spec-V2-helloluno-gift-unboxing.md` — English, 27.5s, brand voice, bookend hook
- `ad-spec-V3-german-weihnachts-33off.md` — German, 35.5s, recommender voice, 33% off + bundle
- `ad-spec-V4-german-scarcity-last-day.md` — German, 10.2s, pure deadline scarcity
- `ad-spec-V5-helloluno-paperwaste.md` — English, 25.1s, brand voice, aspirational close + comment-reply overlay

## What all five have in common (counted)

1. **Single-voice voice-over over demo b-roll, zero talking heads.** 5/5. The viewer never sees a face delivering copy. All persuasion lives in the voice + the glowing-board demo.

2. **Vertical 720×1280, captioned throughout.** 5/5. One persistent top-of-frame caption (promise-shaped) + bottom auto-captions with highlighted keywords.

3. **No exclamation marks in VO.** 5/5. The only "!!!" in the set lives in the V5 comment-reply overlay, not in the voice.

4. **Mechanism = light + reusability, without a proper name.** 4/5 ads (V1, V2, V3, V5) explicitly land both: the stroke lights up + it can be wiped. V4 skips mechanism entirely.

5. **Product named early, brand named late or not at all.** 5/5. The product category ("LED drawing board / Maltafel / Zeichenbrett") enters at 10-18% of VO. The brand name enters at 85%+ or never appears in VO.

6. **Price never stated as an absolute number.** 5/5. Only percentages (33%, 25%), bonus stacks (free pens, free markers, templates), or vague "on sale". Never "$X".

7. **Scarcity is doing heavy lifting.** 4/5 (V1 stock scarcity, V2 limited-time, V3 price-rise, V4 countdown). V5 is the single soft-close in the set.

8. **The gift frame is explicit.** 5/5. Words used: "Christmas gift" (V1, V3), "the gift your kids will love" (V2, V5), "Weihnachtsgeschenk" (V3). All five sell to the gift buyer.

9. **iPad swap or paper-waste objection is the only objection handled.** V1 and V3 use the iPad swap. V2 and V5 use the paper-waste frame. V4 skips objections. 4/5 ads handle exactly one objection.

10. **Hook = promise or proclamation, not problem.** 4/5 open with the gift promise ("This is the gift..."). V4 opens with the deadline (offer-led). None open on pain.

## What differs (and when to use which)

| Structural choice | Options seen | When to use each |
|---|---|---|
| Lead length | 10-17 words (6-17% of VO) | Long creative (V1 at 47s) → short hook, punchier. Short creative (V4 at 10s) → the whole ad IS the hook |
| Narrator identity | 1st-person parent (V1) / brand-recommender (V2, V3, V5) / brand-direct (V4) | Parent voice = cold-traffic belief building. Brand voice = warm/mid-funnel. Brand-direct = retargeting |
| Proof device | On-screen comment overlay (V5) / spoken scarcity precedent (V1 "sold out last year") / countdown visual (V4) | Pick ONE per ad. Stacking all three dilutes each |
| Close | Scarcity CTA (V1, V4) / hook bookend (V2) / soft discount CTA (V3) / aspirational 3-part list (V5) | Cold-traffic → aspirational or bookend. Mid-funnel → scarcity. Retargeting → loss-close |
| Numbers | 0 (V1, V2, V5) / 33/7/44 bundle (V3) / 25% + countdown (V4) | Numbers earn money at the OFFER beat only. Don't spray numbers across benefit beats |
| Brand name in VO | Never (V1, V3, V4, V5) / "helloluno.com" at 85% (V2) | Don't name the brand before the CTA; the gift promise travels further without it |

## The reusable formula (the shared spine)

Based on the 5 specs, the common winning spine for a 25-40s video is:

```
[0-15% ]  PROMISE / PROCLAMATION HOOK: "This is the [OCCASION] gift your [TARGET] will [POSITIVE VERB] and never [NEGATIVE VERB]"
          — product category NOT named yet
          — brand NOT named yet
[10-20%]  NAME + CATEGORY: "It is an [LED PRODUCT CATEGORY] and every [STROKE] lights up [SENSORY ADJECTIVES]"
          — paired with the first glowing-board demo
[20-45%]  CORE EXPERIENCE: "Kids / little ones can't get enough of [ACTION] and watching [RESULT]"
          — longest sentences live here
          — one objection handled (iPad swap or paper-waste pick one)
[45-65%]  REUSABILITY + SECONDARY BENEFIT: "Simply wipe it and start again. Totally reusable. / Also a nice night light"
[65-85%]  OFFER: percentage discount + bonus stack (pens / templates / free shipping)
          — no absolute price
[85-100%] SCARCITY CTA: "Grab it before [LOSS EVENT]" — OR — bookend hook callback (identical opening line repeated)
```

Visual slots that work across every voiceover variant:
1. Opening hero shot of the lit product in a gift / holiday / birthday setting
2. 1-3 unbroken close-ups of a hand drawing on the lit board (seasonal theme drawn: Christmas, birthday, hearts)
3. One "dark reveal" shot (lights off, only the glow — the "magic at night" moment)
4. One wipe-reset shot (for the reuse beat)
5. Final hero / packaging shot for the close

## How to use this for our Glowling launch

We planned two paths:

**A. Reuse the footage + swap the voice over (fastest path to shippable creatives).**
For each voiceover variant we write, pick the matching reference spec:
- "Dinner" angle (C1) → V3 structure (soft discount + benefit stack, informal recommender)
- "Shower" / raw pain angle (C2) → NOT a fit for these swipes. V1-V5 are all gift-framed; our shower angle needs original footage
- "WFH call" angle (C3) → not a direct fit either
- "Bedtime" discovery angle (C4) → V1's "night light" close works as a half-template
- "Gift" angle (C5) → V1 full template is the direct match; V5 for the aspirational close variant

Which ads actually reuse cleanly as voiceover variants: **V1 and V5 for the gift angle, V3 for the discount angle, V4 for the scarcity retargeting cut.**

**B. New footage versions matched to our angles (after static test validates the angles).**
Once we see which of the 5 angles in `research/led-drawing-board/angles-to-test.md` wins on the statics, build a video using the spine above with:
- Shot list from section "Visual slots that work across every voiceover variant"
- Voice-over scripts from each copy's rewritten "longform → 30s" cut (we'll need to compress the 800-1,100-word longforms down to ~100 VO words following the V1 blueprint word budget)

## What we should NOT copy from these swipes

- The brand-less VO — ours needs "Glowling" in the CTA block, not nowhere. Breaks pattern 5, but we're building brand equity and the category is small enough that brand recall matters.
- The on-screen "!!!" testimonials — our Meta ads have `degrees_of_freedom` OPT_OUT on text overlays and we've been strict about no-exclamations copy on everything else. Keep the comment-reply overlay device, but phrase the comment without "!!!".
- Price-rise scarcity ("before they raise the price again"). Our Buy 2, Get 1 FREE is a brand-truth time-bounded offer (Cyber Monday deadline is real), so we use "Ends Cyber Monday" and skip the fake price-rise framing.

## Three lines to end on (per Master Doc output rule)

1. Common spine across all 5 ads: single-voice VO, demo-only video, promise hook, product category named at 10-18%, brand name at 85% or never, no absolute prices, one objection handled, scarcity close.

2. Reuse-friendliest ad for our Gift angle (C5) is V1 — same length, same narrator voice, same beat map.

3. One thing to A/B first on any voiceover rebuild: the hook — Promise ("This is the gift your kids will love and never forget") vs Proclamation ("This is a Christmas gift kids actually want and will use"). Four of the five use one of these two shapes; swap them and run both.
