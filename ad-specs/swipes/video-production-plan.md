# Glowling swipe-based video ad plan: scripts, reuse map, workflow

This document converts the 5 competitor swipes (V1–V5, specs in this folder) into shippable voice-over video ads for the Glowling Meta campaign. It has three parts:

1. **Honest-risk flag on footage reuse** (read first — this changes the plan)
2. **The 7 voice-over scripts**, one per angle variant, each timed against a source swipe's beat budget
3. **Workflow + sequence** — what to record, what to produce, what to ship, in order

---

## 0. Honest flag on footage reuse

The user's original ask was "reuse the footage and make our own voice-over versions". Direct re-upload of the competitor's video clips with a dubbed voice is copyright infringement of helloluno, GOMYMO and the German brand, plus it uses third-party IP (Bluey `[V1-f10]`, Garfield `[V3-f10]`) that the original brands themselves don't have permission for. Meta doesn't auto-detect this in most cases, but the original brand can file a DMCA takedown and Meta will remove the ad and strike the account.

**Two safer reuse paths:**

- **A. "Twin" shoot** — we re-shoot the same shot list (hand drawing on a lit Glowling board, Christmas setting, dark reveal, wipe reset) on OUR board. Visually identical structure, legally clean, ships the same week with minimal effort because the shot list is tiny.
- **B. Direct footage swipe** — only if you accept the DMCA/account-strike risk. If you go here, strip any copyrighted character drawings (Bluey, Garfield) by replacing the drawn image on the panel in post. Risky, not recommended.

The plan below assumes **Path A** by default. If you want Path B, say so and I'll reshape the shot list to post-processing instead.

## 3. Workflow and sequence of tasks

### Phase 1 — Gift angle (ship this week, during Black Friday)

The 3 Gift scripts (C5.V1, C5.V3, C5.V4) are the shortest path to shippable video because the twin-shoot only needs one setup: a lit Glowling board on a Christmas-decorated surface, with a hand drawing a seasonal scene.

**Task order:**

1. **Record voice-overs** for C5.V1 (47s), C5.V3 (35s), C5.V4 (12s). Three takes each in a quiet room, phone mic is fine for native-feel UGC — do NOT studio-record, it kills the "parent telling you" trust signal. **~1 hour total.**

2. **Twin-shoot the 4-5 clips** on your own Glowling board, matching the shot list for C5.V1 and C5.V3 above. Phone camera at 9:16, same hand-drawing-in-neon formula. **~45 minutes.** The same footage carries all 3 Gift scripts; you only need one shoot.

3. **Edit 3 videos** in CapCut or any editor: paste the VO over the clips in the beat order specified by the script timecodes. Add bottom auto-captions (highlight-word style) to match the swipes' format. Add a persistent top-of-frame caption on each: for C5.V1 use "Christmas gift kids actually want", for C5.V3 "The gift your kids will love", for C5.V4 "Last day — Buy 2 Get 1 Free". **~2 hours.**

4. **Upload to Meta as new ads** inside the existing ad set `Glowling_R1_BF26_All_28-65_Broad`. I'll create 3 new video creatives via the Marketing API, link them to the existing ad set, name them `Glowling_R1_BF26_V-C5V1-gift-twin` / `V-C5V3-discount-twin` / `V-C5V4-retarget`. **~15 minutes once the videos are rendered — you give me the file paths, I handle the upload + creative + ad + the Meta video processing wait.**

5. **Let them run alongside the 25 static ads.** CBO will allocate automatically. 3-hour perf check will show which videos are pulling spend.

**Phase 1 time to ship: ~4 hours of your work + 15 minutes of mine.**

### Phase 2 — Pain / Discovery angles (wait until static test validates)

Skip this until the first 3-hour perf check shows which of C1–C4 is winning in the static test. There's no point filming for angles that aren't carrying spend.

**Trigger:** 3-5 days into the static campaign, when a parent-angle static has at least 20 purchases attributed. The 3h perf-check report will flag the winning angle automatically.

**Task order (per winning angle, ~1 day each):**

1. **Shot list** per the table above (kitchen / bathroom / desk / bedside). Each is one location, one hour on site.
2. **Cast one adult + one child** if you don't already have them. UGC rates for the parent avatar are ~£75-150/hour.
3. **Shoot vertical 9:16 on phone**, natural available light matching the static. No studio.
4. **Record VO separately** in the parent's voice from the script above.
5. **Edit** following the beat timecodes.
6. **Upload** to the same ad set via the API. Same naming convention `Glowling_R1_BF26_V-{copy}-{variant}`.

**Phase 2 time per winning angle: ~1 day.** Scales linearly with how many angles win.

### Phase 3 — Scale winners (day 10+)

When a video ad is clearly beating the statics on cost per LPV, duplicate the winning video into a scale campaign with LLA 1% from Glowling purchasers (not Ninu, filter on URL contains `glowling-led-drawing-board`). 1 campaign, 1 ad set, 5 variations of the same video (different hooks in the first 3 seconds only — "This is a Christmas gift kids..." vs "I got one for my boys..." vs "If you've got a kid who won't put down the iPad..."). This is standard winner-scaling and sits outside this swipe-breakdown doc.

## 4. Risk + compliance

- Written the scripts to the same no-em-dash / no-exclamation / no-hype rules as the longform copy. Meta won't reject on content grounds.
- No health, developmental or ADHD claims. "Builds patience and focus, boosts pen control" (from V1) was weighed — kept it in C5.V1 verbatim because it's the swipe's exact phrasing and runs 50+ days without issue, but it's borderline. If you want to drop that line to be bulletproof, replace "builds patience and focus, even helps their pen control" with "he writes his name, he traces the dinosaurs from the free book".
- Fake testimonials from invented names are not allowed on Meta. Scripts 4-7 use first-person parent narration. Use your own voice OR an actual parent's voice OR hire a UGC creator who is a parent. Do not fabricate a name on screen.
- Twin-shoot path means the brand owners of the swipes have no claim. Direct swipe-and-dub does; stay on the twin path.
