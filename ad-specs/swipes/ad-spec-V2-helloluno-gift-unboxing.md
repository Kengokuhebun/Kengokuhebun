## 6 Reusable blueprint

```
[PROMISE-HOOK, 9-12w: "This is the [CATEGORY: gift / thing / routine] your [TARGET] will [POSITIVE VERB] and never [NEGATIVE VERB]"]
[QUALIFY+NAME, 10-15w: "If you're looking for something truly [QUALIFIER], this [PRODUCT] is perfect."]
[SOCIAL-PROOF+BENEFIT, 18-24w, longest sentence of the piece: "It's the star of every [OCCASION] because [TARGET] can't get enough of [CORE ACTION] and watching [RESULT]."]
[OBJECTION-ECO, 10-16w: "And the best part, it's [SPEC], so no more [NEGATIVE OUTCOME]."]
[OFFER+SCARCITY, 15-22w: "Right now we're offering [BONUS 1] and [BONUS 2] with every order, but only for a limited time."]
[CTA, 8-10w, name the destination verbatim: "Available exclusively online at [DOMAIN] or by clicking below."]
[PROMISE-CALLBACK, identical to hook line: repeat verbatim as the final frame]
```

Visual slots (so voice-over swap works on the same footage):
- Beat 1: box being opened (anticipation shot)
- Beat 3: close-up of drawing in action, bright neon on lit panel
- Beat 4: wipe/reset shot
- Beat 5: packaging + markers displayed clearly (for the "with every order" line)
- Beat 7: final hero shot

Never do:
- Never introduce the brand in VO before beat 6
- Never use an exclamation mark
- Never show a face (no talking head)
- Never say the price
- Never add a unique mechanism name — the ad is deliberately low-sophistication
