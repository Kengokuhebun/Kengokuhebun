- AVATAR'S STATE: already Product Aware (knows the drawing pad), needs a final push
- AWARENESS LEVEL: **Product Aware → Most Aware** (highest awareness of the five in this set)

- DOMINANT EMOTION: Loss aversion / urgency. Lines: `[¶1]` "letzte Tag", `[¶2]` "letzte Chance", `[¶3]` "Ab morgen sind die Rabatte weg"
- SECONDARY EMOTIONS: Warmth via visual (smiling child, handmade Mommy / Luna / Eric names). The visual does the emotional work the copy doesn't
- ENEMY / VILLAIN: Time / the deadline. Not an external villain
- UMP: Not present

## 4A Beat map

| # | Label | ¶ | Words | % | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deadline announcement | ¶1 | 17 | 50% | Open on the final-day frame |
| 2 | Direct address + urgency | ¶2 | 10 | 29% | Collapse decision time to "now" |
| 3 | Loss-of-discount close | ¶3 | 7 | 21% | Shortest sentence lands the stakes |

Three-beat accelerating-urgency pattern. Each sentence is shorter than the last (17 → 10 → 7 words).

## 4B Formula line

DEADLINE (17w, 50%) → URGENCY (10w, 29%) → LOSS-CLOSE (7w, 21%)

## 4C Open-loop ledger
Only one loop. Opened in `[¶1]` ("today is the last day"), closed in `[¶3]` ("tomorrow the discounts are gone"). Hold: 24 words.

## 4D Belief-shift ladder
| Step | From | To | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I can think about it" | "Today is literally the last day" | `[¶1]` |
| 2 | "Maybe later" | "Now is the last chance" | `[¶2]` |
| 3 | "What if I wait?" | "Tomorrow the discounts are gone" | `[¶3]` |

Each step tightens the time window. The ad does not create belief about the product — the viewer must already believe it works. All three belief shifts are about time.

## 6 Reusable blueprint

```
[DEADLINE, 14-18w: "This is the last day you can get our [PRODUCT] at [PRICE DESCRIPTOR]."]
[URGENCY, 8-12w: "If you want it, now is your last chance."]
[LOSS-CLOSE, 6-9w: "From [TIMEFRAME] the discounts are gone."]
```

Visual slots:
- One sustained hero shot (ideally a happy child with the product in use, personalised if possible — the "Mommy Luna Eric" angle is the emotional pull of this cut)
- On-screen red countdown button with specific % + "until 00:00"
- No cuts needed — this is a one-shot ad

Never do:
- Never introduce the product in this ad (assumes prior awareness)
- Never list benefits
- Never explain the mechanism
- Never use an exclamation mark in VO
- Never run this as a cold ad — it depends on having already seen the product

When to use:
- As the final 24-72h cut in a sequence after a warmer ad (like V3) has done the belief work
- As a retargeting creative for ATC-abandoned carts in the last hours of a discount window
