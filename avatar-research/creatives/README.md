**Native rules followed:**
- No product, no logo and no faces in the images.
- The oil is pale golden yellow, never dark.
- No hair-loss or scalp close-up imagery, and no before-and-after.
- The photos are AI-generated scenes, made with nano-banana via kie.ai. They are not customer photos, and nothing labels them as customer photos.

**Before you run them:**
1. The test changes two things at once: the angle and the format (colour versus native). If one ad wins, you won't know whether the angle or the format won it. If you want a clean read later, rebuild the winning angle in the other format.
2. The product page still mentions postpartum in about 6 places (`pdp/ninu-pdp.html`). A woman who clicks a menopause ad will land on a page about new mothers. Fix that before spending.
3. Meta policy: the text is all first person ("my hair", "my derm"). Don't change it to "your menopause" or "are you...". Ads can't suggest they know something personal about the viewer's health.
