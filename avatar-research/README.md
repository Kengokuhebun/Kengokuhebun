## 2. How to work with this owner

- Short answers. One best version, ready to use. A recommendation, not a menu. Say plainly what you are unsure of.
- Honest claims only. No fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake reviews, invented customers or invented quotes. No hair regrowth, stopping shedding, medical or "treatment" claims, ever. The compliance lane that has held so far is behaviour and delivery only: "the oil lands on the scalp", "I stopped skipping nights".
- In research: verbatim quotes only, typos kept, every quote linked to the thread or review it came from. Flag thin evidence. Never fill a gap with something you did not read.
- New copy (if asked): no em dashes, no exclamation marks. Say "oiling my scalp", not "doing my scalp oil".
- Do not publish, post, message anyone, spend money or change the store or ad account. Never print or reuse API keys.
- The owner follows a mentor (Aaron) and the master document above. Key rules from it: one variable per test; hooks from three doors (pain, identity, curiosity or mechanism); native-looking creative; real urgency only; validate before correcting the reader.

## 4. What the data says about who buys (the reason for this session)

All 7 orders came from Meta ads (Oct 22 to 30, 2026). Every Ninu ad set targeted women only. Age data below is Meta's (the age on the account that saw the ad).

**Ads that sold (5 ad sets, about £1,069 spent):**

| Age | Spend share | Link clicks | Add to cart | Purchases |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 to 24 | 2.5% | 37 | 0 | 0 |
| 25 to 34 | 6.0% | 81 | 0 | 0 |
| 35 to 44 | 7.0% | 46 | 2 | 1 |
| 45 to 54 | 7.2% | 28 | 0 | 0 |
| 55 to 64 | 20.7% | 43 | 6 | 2 |
| 65+ | 56.5% | 146 | 8 | 4 |

- **6 of 7 purchases and 14 of 16 add-to-carts came from women 55+.** No one under 35 added to cart.
- 5 of 7 orders shipped to the billing address (likely buying for themselves, not as gifts).
- Placements that sold: Facebook Feed (4), Facebook Stories, Instagram Stories, Instagram Reels (1 each).
- Six of the seven orders were a single kit. One order added the oil refill.

**What did not sell:** every ad since Oct 29 that got cheap clicks (£0.15 to £0.28) from younger audiences on Reels: about 2,800 clicks, 2 add-to-carts, 0 orders. Their hooks led with the baby ("Baby up three times. Wet hands, in bed. Skipped my scalp oil again.").

**Retest on Nov 10 to 11:** the same three selling ads, aimed at women 35 to 65+ on Feed and Stories only, £80.46 spent, 31 clicks, 0 add-to-carts. Small sample, but no repeat yet. Meta also prices two of the three selling ads very high (£266 to £327 per 1,000 impressions), which suggests the people who respond to them are a narrow, expensive audience.

**Read this as hypotheses to test, not facts:**
- The 55+ buyer may be oiling her scalp for age-related or menopausal thinning, not postpartum shedding.
- She may respond to the messy, skipped routine and the delivery mechanism, and not to the baby details.
- The ad copy that sold her still mentions a baby. Find out why it worked anyway: did the routine pain carry it?
- Other possibilities the research should check: grey-haired or thinning-at-the-part women, women with hand or grip issues (one-handed, no mess), women who already bought rosemary oil because of social media and stopped using it.

## 5. The desire and angle that converted

**The ad that produced all 7 sales ("Ad C", the messy routine story).** It opens: "I actually do my scalp oil every night now. That wasn't true six months ago." Headline: "Your messy oil routine's worst nightmare". Full copy: `ads/ninu-r8-hook-only.md`.

- **Desire:** "I was never the problem. The method was." / "I just wanted to feel like myself again."
- **Angle:** habit and delivery. "The habit was always there. The routine just needed to be easier than the reason to quit."
- **Mechanism:** hair does not absorb oil, skin does. A dropper or fingers put most of it on the hair, neck and towel. Hollow tips press along the part and release it on the scalp.
- **Failed solutions in the copy:** castor oil, rosemary oil, droppers, fingers, a towel on the shoulders, "I'll start over Sunday".
- **Awareness and sophistication (earlier estimate, for the postpartum avatar):** awareness 3 (knows the problem, knows oiling, does not know the delivery issue), sophistication 3. Re-check both for the new avatar.

Use this desire as the thread into Reddit and reviews: who keeps abandoning scalp oiling, why, and what they say about it.
